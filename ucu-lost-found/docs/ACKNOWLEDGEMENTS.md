## AI assistance

_Declare this only as permitted by the examination rules, and edit it to match what your group actually did._

The group used **Claude (Anthropic)**, an AI assistant, to help draft parts of the code, tests, diagrams and documentation. Every part was reviewed, run and tested by the group, and each member can explain the parts they are responsible for. Design decisions (private identifying details, server-side sessions, a database-level single-approval rule) were discussed and accepted by the group.

## Fictional data

All names, student numbers, phone numbers, email addresses and events in the sample data are invented for demonstration. Campus place names are used only to make the scenario realistic.
